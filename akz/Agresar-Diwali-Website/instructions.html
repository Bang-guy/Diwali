Play Game instructions

BEFORE YOU TAKE YOUR TURN

Know the games

Ten challenges, plenty of laughs. Here's how each one works.

01

Diya Tower

Two teams of three people compete. Each team builds a tower using diyas. The team that builds the tallest tower in the shortest time wins.

PLAYERS · 2 TEAMS OF 3
02

Dumb Charades

The host places a prompt card on the contestant's forehead. People in the audience act out the clue or give the agreed dialogue clue. The contestant gets two minutes and must guess three prompts correctly to win.

PLAYER · 1 CONTESTANT + AUDIENCE
03

Repeat After Me

The host gives the contestant tongue twisters, tricky words or phrases to repeat. The challenge lasts three minutes. Make it through the full three minutes without getting tricked or making a mistake to win.

TIME · 3 MINUTES
04

Read My Lips

An audience member writes a word or sentence on a chit. The host reads it silently with clear lip movements. The contestant watches the host's lips and tries to guess the word or sentence.

PLAYER · 1 CONTESTANT + 1 AUDIENCE MEMBER
05

Wrong Answers Only

The host asks simple questions, such as “What is 2 + 2?” The contestant must answer incorrectly. Giving the correct answer means the contestant loses.

RULE · CORRECT ANSWER = LOSS
06

Emotion Switch

The host gives a sentence with an emotion, such as happiness or sadness. The contestant must say the same sentence using a different, opposite emotion. For example, deliver “I got a new job!” angrily instead of happily.

CHALLENGE · CHANGE THE EMOTION
07

Character Acting

The contestant acts like a character suggested by the host or audience. Examples: an old granny, or a child who did not get the toy they wanted. Make the character believable and entertaining.

CHALLENGE · ACT IT OUT
08

Blow Candle

Ten candles are arranged in a line on a long table. The contestant gets one attempt to blow out all ten candles. Blow out all ten in that one attempt to win.

CHANCE · ONE ATTEMPT
09

Fill Water Cup

Contestants take turns pouring water into a glass. If a contestant spills water, they lose that turn and receive a truth or dare suggested by the audience.

RULE · SPILLING WATER = TRUTH OR DARE
10

One-Word Story

The participant who gets this game chooses five people. Each person contributes exactly one word at a time, building one shared story together. Keep the story going and see where it leads!

PLAYERS · 5 PEOPLE

Play fair, cheer for everyone, and most importantly, have fun.
